Pitch is represented by "Pitch" with "hold", "step" and "octave"
Time is represented outside of pitch as "Duration"


The Weakness of the MusicXML approach is it is harder to learn for a beginner as you cannot see what is being changed as you change it therefore it is a lot of trial and error when trying to get things right. However, a strength of MusicXML is the layout, as it is very simple when actually using and changing pieces of music.


In the MEI File:
Pitch is represented by "pname", "oct" and "stem.dire"
Time is represented as "dur"


The strengths of this approach is you can see what you are changing in real time as you adjust the identity of notes, this tool is far superior to MusicXML and makes this a much more efficient software. It is also easier to locate code with MEI as when you click on a specific note you want to look at, you get brought to that notes code and it is highlighted. I personally did not find any weakness in MEI as it completed its job perfectly as far as I needed it to.
